# KirubAI

**Turn the words you know into words you use.**

KirubAI is a production-grade AI-powered English vocabulary and conversation learning platform that transforms passive vocabulary into active, usable language skills.

## Problem

Users can understand basic English and discover new words while reading, but they:
- Forget words shortly after learning them
- Recognize words but cannot recall them when needed
- Understand meanings but cannot use words naturally in conversation
- Continue relying on simple vocabulary despite knowing more complex words

**Core Problem:** Converting passive vocabulary into active vocabulary.

## Solution

KirubAI solves this through:
- AI-powered contextual explanations
- Real-life scenario-based practice
- Active recall exercises
- Intelligent spaced repetition
- Natural conversation practice
- Personalized learning paths
- Progress tracking and analytics

- Docker + Docker Compose
